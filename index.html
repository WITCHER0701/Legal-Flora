About Legal Flora

Business paperwork and advice in one place

We are a Jaipur-based consultancy for startups, MSMEs and NGOs. We help with registration, licences, government scheme applications, legal paperwork, marketing, and basic website setup.

We start by understanding what you are trying to set up. Then we explain the documents, fees and steps involved.

For government funding applications, we help prepare the required documents from start to finish. The relevant government authority decides whether to approve the application and release funds. We cannot guarantee either outcome.
