How we use your details

To request a consultation, we collect your name, email address and phone number so our team can contact you. We store the request in our admin dashboard. The form does not verify your email address. You can also contact us directly at info@legalflora.com.

We use temporary browser tab storage to show the consultation prompt only once per visit. After a successful request, we also save a yes/no marker in this browser, without your contact details, so we do not ask you to fill the form again. The marker stays until you clear browser data; a one-year first-party cookie is used as a fallback.

Optional site analytics

We ask before counting page visits and clicks on contact links. We store your yes or no choice with a random ID for your browser tab and the time you chose. If you say yes, we also save page visits and contact-link clicks. Your choice is kept in a first-party cookie for up to 180 days. You can change it through “Cookie choices” in the website footer. Saying no does not affect the contact form.

Admin login

Staff who sign in to the admin dashboard receive a session cookie. It is used to keep the dashboard signed in and expires after eight hours. We also record the admin email, time and whether a sign-in attempt succeeded.

Questions or removal

For questions about an enquiry you submitted, or to ask us to remove it, email info@legalflora.com.
